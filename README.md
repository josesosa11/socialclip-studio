# SocialClip Studio

**An OpusClip alternative with one flat monthly price, no credits.** Turn long videos, podcasts and streams into vertical short clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and X.

👉 **[socialclipstudio.com](https://socialclipstudio.com)**

## How it works

1. **Bring your video**: paste a YouTube, Twitch, Kick or public Facebook link, upload a file, or import from Google Drive.
2. **Get clips**: our AI picks the best moments, cuts them to 9:16 and burns in captions.
3. **Post them**: publish or schedule straight to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, or download.

## Features

- **23 caption styles** on every plan
- **Smart Reframe** frames by who is on screen: one person full screen, two stacked top and bottom, three in rows
- **Twitch and Kick VOD import**, with gaming facecam layouts
- **Silence and filler-word removal**, auto-zoom, background music
- **Schedule All**: queue every clip from a video in one pass
- **Caption translation** into 29 languages (Pro)
- **Caption my Shorts**: upload a finished short and get captions timed to the words
